The combo breaker: people are already planning the day off

Nobody publishes how many people booked that Thursday off. So we measured what people search for when they're planning to skip work, against the same weeks last year. US Google searches for skipping work, 1–14 Nov 2026 against 2–15 Nov 2025: call in sick +40.4% · day off work +73.4% · sick day +36.4%. The Day-Off Index, the average of the three: +50.1%.

Questions people ask

How much did the world produce between GTA V and GTA 6?
About $1.14 quadrillion of goods and services in real terms (constant 2015 US$), over the 4,811 days from GTA V's release (17 September 2013) to the day before GTA 6 (18 November 2026). In 2026 the world makes about $3.3 million a second, or $282 billion a day. World Bank world GDP; 2026 is the World Bank's 2025 figure grown by the IMF's 2026 world growth forecast.
Is that in today's dollars?
No. It's real output at 2015 prices (constant 2015 US$), so years compare fairly. In today's prices the figure would be larger. The per-second rate is a yearly average spread evenly; nobody measures GDP by the second.
Why these milestones?
They're moments most people remember, not a ranking, each dated to a UTC day and backed by one public source, listed on the scoreboard. Each one reads what the world had made since GTA V by the day before it. For example, First image of a black hole: $435T; First COVID-19 vaccine shot outside a trial: $575T; Starship upper stage caught by the tower: $1,140T.
How much more productive did the world get between GTA V and GTA 6?
World real output grew by about a quarter between GTA V (2013) and GTA 6 (2026): GDP per person (constant 2015 US$) +26.3% (2013 to 2026); GDP per worker (constant 2021 PPP $) +24.9% (2013 to 2025). It went up every year except 2020. World Bank data; 2026 is the World Bank's 2025 figure grown by the IMF's 2026 world growth forecast.
Are Americans searching for sick days more before GTA 6?
Yes. US Google searches for skipping work were up 50% on average, 1–14 Nov 2026 against 2–15 Nov 2025: “day off work” +73.4%, “call in sick” +40.4%, “sick day” +36.4%. GTA 6 launches on Thursday 19 November 2026.
What is the Day-Off Index?
The average year-over-year change in US Google searches for “call in sick”, “sick day” and “day off work”. Each phrase compares its average weekly Google Trends interest over two weeks with the same weeks a year earlier. It is always shown with its breakdown.
Did GTA 6 cause the rise?
We can't say. The rise lines up with the run-up to GTA 6, but this is an association, not proof of cause. Search interest has other drivers too.
Is this how many people are taking the day off?
No. It measures Google search interest (a 0–100 index), not a headcount. People who book time off in an HR system don't search for it.
How was it built?
From public Google Trends data, with a dbt project and a MetricFlow semantic layer that Dex wrote, tested and verifies on every refresh. The repository is open source and every number can be reproduced from it.

Ask it yourself

Every number here is a governed metric in a dbt semantic layer that Dex wrote and verified. Clone the repo and ask:

git clone https://github.com/exmergo/gta6-productivity-impact
cd gta6-productivity-impact
uv sync
uv run dbt parse
uv run dex explore semantic query world_output_produced
uv run dex explore semantic query wait_milestone_output --group-by milestone__title,milestone__event_date
uv run dex explore semantic query world_productivity_change_since_gta_v,world_productivity --group-by world_productivity_key__measure,world_productivity_key__data_year
uv run dex explore semantic query day_off_index --group-by day_off_index_window__breakdown
uv run dex explore semantic query day_off_index_term_change,day_off_index_term_prior,day_off_index_term_current --group-by day_off_index_term__term
